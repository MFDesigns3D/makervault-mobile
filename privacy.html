Privacy policy

MakerVault and MakerVault Mobile · Effective November 14, 2026

Who we are

MakerVault is a Windows app and a phone web app (MakerVault Mobile, at mfdesigns3d.github.io/makervault-mobile) made by MF Designs 3D in Ottawa, Canada. This policy covers both.

What MakerVault can access in your Google account

Signing in with Google is only needed for sync. When you choose to sign in, MakerVault asks Google for:

  • Your email address (the “openid” and “email” permissions), to show which account is signed in.
  • Google Drive, files MakerVault created (the “drive.file” permission). This only lets MakerVault see and change files it created itself. It cannot see your other Drive files, Gmail, contacts or photos.

What MakerVault keeps, and where

Your library (projects, file links, filament, supplies, machines and print log) is kept on your own devices. If you turn on sync, copies of those changes and small thumbnail pictures are saved in a folder called “MakerVault Sync” in your own Google Drive so your other devices can read them. MakerVault does not run a server, and none of this is sent to MF Designs 3D or to anyone else.

How Google user data is used

Data from Google is used only to sync your own MakerVault library between your own devices. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with other people or companies, and not used to train AI models. No person at MF Designs 3D reads it.

MakerVault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Camera, NFC and files on your computer

The phone camera and NFC are used only while you scan, and what they read stays on the phone. The Windows app reads only the folders you add, and only moves a file to the Windows Recycle Bin when you confirm it in the app.

No ads, no tracking

MakerVault has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. These web pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs.

Security

On Windows, your Google sign-in is stored encrypted with your Windows account. On the phone, it is kept in that browser's own storage for this site. Connections to Google use HTTPS.

Removing your data

Sign out on MakerVault's Sync page (Windows) or with “Sign out of Google” (phone). To remove the synced copy, delete the “MakerVault Sync” folder in your Google Drive. To take away MakerVault's access, go to myaccount.google.com/connections and remove MakerVault. Your library on each computer stays until you remove it there.

Children

MakerVault is not made for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. Questions: contact@mfdesigns3d.com.
