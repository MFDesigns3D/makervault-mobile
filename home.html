MakerVault

One place for a maker's files and workshop: 3D print files, SVGs and pictures with thumbnails, filament rolls, supplies and a print log. It runs on your Windows computer and on your phone.

Open MakerVault MobileRead the privacy policy

Visual file library

The Windows app shows your 3MF, STL, STEP, SVG and image files as pictures, finds duplicates and groups files into projects. It never moves or deletes your files unless you confirm it.

Filament and workshop

Keep track of filament rolls, supply drawers, machines and every print. On the phone, scan a barcode or tap a tag to open a roll.

Sync through your own Google Drive

Your laptop, tablet and phone stay in step through a folder in your own Google Drive. There is no MakerVault server.

Why MakerVault asks you to sign in with Google

MakerVault saves its sync files in a folder called “MakerVault Sync” in your Google Drive. Google's “drive.file” permission lets it see only the files MakerVault created itself, never the rest of your Drive. Your email address is shown so you know which account is signed in. Nothing is sent to anyone else. Details are in the privacy policy.
